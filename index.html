1. Multiplicative AT&C Formula: AT&C Loss is mathematically calculated as 100% − (Billing Efficiency × Collection Efficiency). It is strictly multiplicative, not additive.

2. Energy & Financial Units: Input and Sold Energy are stored in MWh in PostgreSQL and displayed in MU (Million Units = 1,000 MWh). Assessment and Realisation are stored in ₹ Lakhs and displayed in ₹ Crore (100 Lakhs = 1 Crore).

3. Average Billing Rate (ABR): Calculated as Total Assessment (₹) ÷ Total Sold Energy (kWh). Statewide average is ₹43.32/kWh.

4. Excluded Categories: Feeders with zero input energy (233) or no consumers tagged in August (1,515) are tracked in the baseline network but excluded from active audit slabs.
