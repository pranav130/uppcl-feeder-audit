1. Multiplicative AT&C Formula: AT&C Loss is mathematically calculated as 100% − (Billing Efficiency × Collection Efficiency).

2. Thru Rate vs ABR: ABR (Average Billing Rate) = Assessment ÷ Sold Energy. Thru Rate = Realised Amount ÷ Input Energy, indicating actual cash realized per unit wheeled.

3. Leave-out Filters: Abnormal Assessment excludes feeders with assessment > ₹1 Crore and ABR > ₹50/unit. Billed > Input removes feeders with negative line loss. Zero input leaves out feeders without metering in August.

4. Units: Energy in MU (Million Units = 1,000 MWh). Financial amounts in ₹ Crore (100 Lakhs = 1 Crore). Rates in ₹/kWh.
